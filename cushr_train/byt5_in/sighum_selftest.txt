cikṣepa me suto rājan sa gatāsur athāpatat
ahaṃ saubhapateḥ senām āyasair bhujagair iva
chinnān dṛṣṭvā tu tān bāṇān pradyumnena sa saubharāṭ
śakyaḥ śūrasuto hantum api vajrabhṛtā svayam
rūpaṃ pitur apaśyaṃ tacchakuneḥ patato yathā
kṣurāntam amalaṃ cakraṃ kālāntakayamopamam
viśrambhaghātakānāṃ tu prayāge śṛṇu yatphalam
evaṃ yogasya samprāptisthānaṃ paramadurlabham
pradiṣṭāni ca deyāni na dadyur bhartṛśāsanāt
somadatto yuyutsuś ca droṇaputras tathaiva ca
paśya māyāprabhāvo 'yam īśvareṇa yathā kṛtaḥ
anyathaiva hi manyante puruṣās tāni tāni ca
karmaṇā tena pāpena lipyate nūnam īśvaraḥ
aśaṅkamānaḥ kalyāṇi so 'mutrānantyam aśnute
kurvato nārthasiddhir me bhavatīti ha bhārata
snehasvedapūrvakair vamanādibhir doṣo yena saḥ
māghamāse gamiṣyanti gaṅgāyāṃ bharatarṣabha
dharmakāme pratītasya pratipannāḥ sma bhārata
yasya dharmo hi dharmārthaṃ kleśabhāṅna sa paṇḍitaḥ
mitrāṇi tasya naśyanti dharmārthābhyāṃ ca hīyate
